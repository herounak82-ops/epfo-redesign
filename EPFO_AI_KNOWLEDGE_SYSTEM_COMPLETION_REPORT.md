## Validation

`12_VALIDATION/validate_knowledge_system.py` completed with **PASS**, 0 errors, 81 checks, 30 routes, 14 GIGW rules, 5 retrieval simulations, and 88 manifest paths. XML and JSON parsing, stable canonical IDs, route targets, raw-source exclusion from defaults, GIGW source mappings, manifest paths, and simulation completeness were checked.

This is a knowledge-system validation result. It does not claim browser validation, production deployment, formal accessibility certification, real EPFO backend integration, real authentication, or statutory eligibility verification.

## Unresolved conflicts and limitations

No blocking structural conflicts remain. The source corpus has known limitations: the supplied UX4G dossier is research input rather than a live package guarantee; the GIGW page numbers are not available in the extracted source and therefore are not fabricated; formal grievance volumes are prioritization evidence rather than pure UX-defect prevalence; and a complete production EPFO schema or internal service behavior is not established. These limitations are recorded in the relevant source, research, and freshness files.
