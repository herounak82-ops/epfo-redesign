# Negative Retrieval Rules

Do not preload unrelated portfolio knowledge, personal biography information, historical design experiments, unrelated visual references, raw archives, complete UX4G dossier, complete GIGW text, unrelated journey data, or unrelated implementation repositories. For a claim task, do not preload transfer research. For visual refinement, do not preload backend architecture. For typography, do not preload the GIGW cybersecurity chapter. For a GIGW audit, do not preload portfolio references. For Codex work, do not let assistant-specific work rewrite the design system.
