# EPFO Product Model

The product is a synthetic member-service prototype organized around five goals: get PF money, fix personal details, move PF after a job change, view account, and fix a failed request. The product should make administrative dependencies visible without asking members to understand internal form numbers first.

| Goal | Primary journey | Core outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Get PF money | claim | Understand eligibility/progress and submit or recover a claim. |
| Fix personal details | KYC | See which identity item needs action and who owns it. |
| Move PF after a job change | transfer | Understand source/destination employment and transfer status. |
| View account | access/account health | See balances, employments, KYC, and service health together. |
| Fix a failed request | failure recovery | Diagnose the failure and take the next supported action. |

The assistant is not the product model. It may later provide another interface over this model, but every normal UI journey must remain independently usable.
