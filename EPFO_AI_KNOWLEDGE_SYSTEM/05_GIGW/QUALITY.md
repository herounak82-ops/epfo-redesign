# GIGW Quality

EPFO interpretation: service quality is visible when members can understand where they are, what a service means, who owns a blocker, what to do next, and how to obtain help. Implementation should use goal-based navigation, plain content, explicit service states, ownership/contact information, feedback routes, responsive behavior, and current update/maintenance visibility. Evaluation should test representative member goals rather than only visual conformance.

Relevant rules: `GIGW-Q-001`, `GIGW-Q-002`, `GIGW-Q-003`, `GIGW-Q-004`.
