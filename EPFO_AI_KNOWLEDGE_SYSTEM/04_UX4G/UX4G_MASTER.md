# UX4G Knowledge Master

UX4G is treated as a government UX/design system with five connected layers: design language, component library, service-pattern library, practice/governance, and implementation ecosystem. Current project guidance identifies foundations including color, typography, spacing/layout, elevation, iconography, design tokens, accessibility, and content design; it also emphasizes WCAG 2.1 AA, keyboard operation, screen-reader support, focus management, plain language, multilingual support, mobile-first design, progressive disclosure, clear feedback, and predictable navigation. These are normalized here as derived records, not copied as a dossier.

UX4G and GIGW are complementary. UX4G answers how the service experience and components should be designed and implemented. GIGW answers government quality, accessibility, cybersecurity, lifecycle, and evaluation expectations. EPFO project decisions apply both to the current prototype.
